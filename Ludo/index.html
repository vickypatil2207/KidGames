      🌟 How to Play 🌟

      👋 Welcome to Ludo Party! Here are the rules for little champions:

      • ✨ Releasing a Token: Roll a 6 to free your cartoon token from the corner yard and put it on the starting star cell!
      • 🎲 Extra Rolls: If you roll a 6, you get to roll the dice one more time!
      • ⚡ Moving: Tap/click on your glowing token to move it forward. If it's a computer's turn, it will choose and move automatically!
      • 💥 Cute Capture: Land on an opponent's token to "tag" it, sending it back to their yard, and get an extra roll!
      • ⭐ Safe Cells: Tokens are safe from being tagged on any cell marked with a star!
      • 🏆 Landing Home: Race all 4 of your tokens all the way around the board and into the center home triangle to win!
